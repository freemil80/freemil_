Global Trends and Research Gaps in Anaerobic Digestion: A Systematic and Bibliometric Review with Implications for Ghana

Darmey, J., Narra, S., Achaw, O.W., Stinner, W., Frimpong, I.K., Amoatey, N., Agyekum, T.O. & Amaniampong, D. Global Trends and Research Gaps in Anaerobic Digestion: A Systematic and Bibliometric Review with Implications for Ghana. Environments, 13(7), 408. MDPI.

View research focus

Research focus

Systematic and bibliometric review of anaerobic digestion trends, research gaps, and implications for Ghana.

Research approach
  • Reviewed scholarly research on anaerobic digestion.
  • Examined publication trends and major research themes.
  • Identified gaps with relevance to Ghana.
My contribution
  • Helped develop the research methodology and review framework.
  • Collected article metadata from Scopus using Boolean search strings.
  • Cleaned the dataset and removed records that did not meet the research criteria.
  • Conducted bibliometric analysis using VOSviewer.
  • Contributed to writing the research methodology.
  • Helped address reviewers’ comments.
  • Reviewed the final manuscript draft.
Research significance

The review supports evidence-based discussion around waste management, renewable energy, and sustainable development opportunities in Ghana.
