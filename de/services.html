Leistungen

Forschung, Datenanalyse und wissenschaftliche Unterstützung.

I support students, researchers, teams, and organizations with services that connect scientific thinking with practical data work.

01

Datenanalyse

Cleaning, organizing, analyzing, and interpreting datasets for research and decision-making.

Typische Ergebnisse

  • Cleaned and organized datasets
  • Analysis summary with practical insights
  • Supporting charts or visuals
02

Power-BI-Dashboard

Interactive dashboards for tracking trends, communicating insights, and presenting results clearly.

Typische Ergebnisse

  • Interactive Power BI dashboard
  • Data model and dashboard structure
  • Short walkthrough of key insights
03

Forschungsunterstützung

Assistance with research planning, data interpretation, documentation, and scientific reporting.

Typische Ergebnisse

  • Research planning and documentation
  • Data interpretation support
  • Scientific reporting guidance
04

Systematische Übersichtsarbeit

Support for PRISMA-style review design, screening workflows, bibliometric mapping, and synthesis.

Typische Ergebnisse

  • Search and screening workflow
  • PRISMA-ready review records
  • Bibliometric or evidence synthesis support
05

Wissenschaftliches Schreiben

Help with abstracts, reports, manuscripts, technical writing, and communicating complex findings.

Typische Ergebnisse

  • Abstracts, reports, or manuscript support
  • Technical structure and editing
  • Clear communication of scientific findings
06

Zusammenarbeit

Collaborative support for multidisciplinary research, data projects, scientific initiatives, and sustainability-focused work.

Typische Ergebnisse

  • Defined collaboration scope
  • Shared research or data workflow
  • Project support plan for next steps
